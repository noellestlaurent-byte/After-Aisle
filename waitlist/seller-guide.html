Seller Onboarding & Shipping Guide

Everything you need to know to list, ship, and sell on After Aisle — so your decor finds its next home beautifully.

01 — Listing Standards

What We Accept

After Aisle is a vetted, editorial marketplace. Every listing is reviewed by our team before it goes live. We only accept items that meet our condition and aesthetic standards.

Accepted Item Categories

  • Arch frames, ceremony structures, and backdrops
  • Candle holders, candlesticks, and lanterns
  • Table linens, runners, and overlays
  • Centerpiece vessels, urns, and floral stands
  • Custom signage frames and easels
  • Premium tabletop (chargers, glassware, flatware)
  • Bridal accessories and editorial props

Condition Requirements

All items must be listed under one of the following condition tiers:

Pristine

Used once or never used. No visible wear, scratches, chips, or staining. Appears as new.

Excellent

Used 1–2 times. Minor surface wear only — not visible in standard photos. Fully functional and display-ready.

Good

Used multiple times. Light wear consistent with use (small scuffs, minor patina). Any imperfections must be clearly photographed and disclosed in the listing description.

For a full breakdown of what is and isn't accepted on After Aisle, see What won't be accepted on After Aisle? on our FAQ.

Listing Details — Required Fields

Every listing must include accurate packaged dimensions and weight before submission. This is how shipping costs are calculated automatically for buyers at checkout — missing or inaccurate measurements will generate incorrect shipping estimates, which creates a poor buyer experience and potential disputes.

  • Packaged dimensions — height, width, and depth in inches of the item as it will be shipped, including the box. If you haven't packaged it yet, estimate by adding approximately 2 inches to each dimension of the item to account for the box and padding.
  • Packaged weight — total weight in pounds of the item plus anticipated packaging materials. Add approximately 2–3 lbs to account for bubble wrap, box, and packing materials. When in doubt, round up — underestimating weight creates shipping cost gaps that fall on you as the seller.

If your item is best suited for local pickup — arch frames and large structures in particular, or if you don't want to deal with shipping — note this in your listing description. Local pickup eliminates shipping costs for the buyer entirely.

02 — Listing Photography

Photo Requirements

Great photography is the single most important factor in selling your items. After Aisle is an editorial marketplace — your photos should reflect that.

Required Shots (minimum 5 photos per listing)

  • Full item shot — entire piece visible, neutral or lifestyle background
  • Detail shot — close-up of texture, material, or craftsmanship
  • Any wear or imperfection — required if condition is Good; strongly recommended for Excellent
  • Professional wedding photo — at least one image of the item styled and in use at your actual wedding, taken by your wedding photographer
  • Timestamped photo — a recent photo of the item with a visible timestamp or screenshot showing the date taken, so buyers can verify current condition

Photography Tips

  • Shoot in natural light — avoid harsh flash or yellow indoor lighting
  • Use a clean, neutral background (white wall, cream linen, marble surface)
  • Shoot on a high-resolution phone camera — portrait mode adds polish
  • Do not use heavy filters — buyers need accurate color representation

03 — Packaging Standards

How to Pack Your Items

Sellers are responsible for all packaging. Items that arrive damaged due to poor packaging are the seller's liability. Follow these standards to protect yourself and your buyer.

General Rules

  • All items must be wrapped in bubble wrap or equivalent cushioning material
  • Use a box with at least 2 inches of padding on all sides
  • Fragile items (glassware, candle holders, ceramic vessels) must be double-boxed
  • Multiple items in one order must be individually wrapped before being placed together
  • Seal all boxes with packing tape on all seams — not just the top flap

By Item Type

◇

Arch Frames & Large Structures

Disassemble completely. Wrap each component individually in bubble wrap. Use a heavy-duty double-walled box or PVC pipe carriers. Include all hardware and assembly instructions if available. Note: Due to their size and fragility, arch frames and large structures are often best sold as local pickup. We encourage sellers to consider this option when listing.

◇

Candle Holders & Glass Items

Wrap each piece in a minimum of 3 layers of bubble wrap. Double-box with packing peanuts or foam fill between boxes. Mark the outer box "FRAGILE" on all sides.

◇

Linens & Fabric Items

Fold neatly and place in a sealed plastic bag before boxing. This protects against moisture during transit. No need for bubble wrap — focus on keeping clean and dry.

◇

Tabletop (Chargers, Glassware, Flatware)

Wrap each piece individually. Stack plates with cardboard dividers. Use cell kits (divided boxes) for glassware when possible. Double-box.

◇

Signage Frames & Easels

Disassemble if possible. Wrap corners and edges with extra bubble wrap — these are the most vulnerable points. Use a flat box sized to the frame; avoid rolling or forcing into undersized packaging.

Pro tip: Take a photo of your packed item before sealing the box. This protects you in any damage dispute — proof that the item was properly packaged at the time of shipment.

04 — Shipping Requirements

Shipping Your Items

Carrier Recommendations

  • UPS or FedEx — strongly recommended for fragile and large items; better handling than USPS for delicate decor
  • USPS Priority Mail — acceptable for small, non-fragile items (linens, accessories)
  • Freight — required for very large structures (full arch frames over 6ft, large furniture-style pieces)

Shipping Timeline

  • All orders must be shipped within 5 business days of a confirmed sale
  • Tracking information must be uploaded to the platform within 24 hours of shipping
  • Orders not shipped within 5 days may be cancelled and refunded to the buyer

Shipping Insurance

  • Shipping insurance is required for all items over $200
  • For items under $200, insurance is strongly recommended but not mandatory
  • In the event of carrier damage on an insured item, After Aisle will assist with the claim process
  • Uninsured items damaged in transit are the seller's financial responsibility

Tracked shipping only. Orders shipped without tracking are not covered by After Aisle seller protection. Always ship with a method that provides end-to-end tracking.

05 — Payment & Escrow Policy

How You Get Paid

After Aisle uses a 48-hour buyer inspection window to protect both parties. Here's exactly how it works:

01

Item sells

Buyer's payment is collected and held in escrow by After Aisle.

02

You ship within 5 business days

Upload tracking to the platform within 24 hours of shipping.

03

Buyer receives the item

The 48-hour inspection window begins at the time of confirmed delivery.

04

No dispute filed within 48 hours

Funds are automatically released from escrow to your account. Sale is considered final.

05

Dispute filed within 48 hours

After Aisle mediates. Buyer must provide photos of the item and packaging. Resolution within 3 business days.

Commission

After Aisle takes a 20% commission on each completed sale. You keep 80% of your listing price.

Founding sellers (first 25) pay zero commission, forever — you keep 100% of every sale.

06 — Disputes & Damage

If Something Goes Wrong

Buyer Opens a Dispute

If a buyer files a dispute within the 48-hour window, After Aisle will:

  • Request photos from the buyer of the item and all packaging materials
  • Contact you for your account of how the item was packed and shipped
  • Review all evidence and issue a decision within 3 business days

Outcome Scenarios

Item not as described

If the item significantly differs from the listing (wrong item, undisclosed damage, wrong condition tier), the buyer receives a full refund. This amount is deducted from your payout. The buyer returns the item at your expense.

Damage due to poor packaging

If photos indicate inadequate packaging caused the damage, the seller is responsible for a partial or full refund at After Aisle's discretion.

Carrier damage (insured item)

After Aisle will assist both parties in filing a carrier insurance claim. Funds remain in escrow during the claims process.

Carrier damage (uninsured item)

Seller bears financial responsibility. This is why insurance is required on items over $200.

Dispute not resolved in seller's favor (repeat)

Two unresolved disputes within 90 days will result in a review of your seller account. Three may result in removal from the platform.

Our goal is always a fair resolution. After Aisle mediates every dispute personally — you will always speak to a real person, not a bot. Reach out to afteraisle@gmail.com at any time.

07 — Pre-Ship Checklist

Before Every Shipment

Run through this list before sealing the box on every order.

  • Item matches the listing photos and condition description exactly
  • Item is clean and free of any debris, wax, or residue
  • Every piece is individually wrapped in bubble wrap
  • Box has at least 2 inches of padding on all sides
  • Fragile items are double-boxed
  • All seams are taped — not just the top flap
  • Shipping insurance purchased (required for items over $200)
  • Photo taken of packed item before sealing
  • Tracked shipping method selected
  • Tracking number uploaded to After Aisle within 24 hours of drop-off

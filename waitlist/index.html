Questions

What is After Aisle?

After Aisle is a curated, national marketplace designed for the modern, editorial-standard bride. We provide a sophisticated platform to buy and resell high-end wedding decor, transforming "sunk costs" into "liquid investments." Think of us as the RealReal for your wedding aesthetic.

Who is the After Aisle bride?

She is intentional, sophisticated, and tech-forward. She values high-end curation over marketplace chaos and is looking for a streamlined "exit strategy" for her wedding assets without sacrificing her aesthetic.

How is After Aisle different from Facebook Marketplace or eBay?

Curation and trust. We move away from the "digital junkyard" model. Every listing on After Aisle is vetted to meet our editorial standards. We handle the secure transactions and logistics so you don't have to deal with "is this available" messages or risky local pickups.

What kind of items can I sell?

We focus on high quality wedding decor and accessories. This includes linens, architectural candle holders, designer bridal accessories, custom signage frames, and premium tabletop rentals. We're curating different aesthetics. Waitlist members help shape our opening inventory.

How do I list my items?

Once our MVP launches, the process will be seamless. You'll upload high-resolution photos, provide item details, and our team will vet the listing for quality and aesthetic alignment before it goes live to our national community of brides.

When does the platform officially launch?

We are currently in the "Build Phase". By joining our waitlist, you'll get early access to our Beta launch and exclusive updates on our progress.

How can I stay updated on the build?

Follow our founder, Noelle, on TikTok @afteraisle for a "Building in Public" look at how we are modernizing the wedding industry, one curated listing at a time.
